# Code of conduct

agent-build follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep
the discussion about the work.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[a private report](https://github.com/magnoscg/agent-build/security/advisories/new),
saying in its title that it is a conduct report. Every report is reviewed,
and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues, and may
warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, as the Contributor Covenant
describes.
